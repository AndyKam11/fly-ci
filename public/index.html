What is this?

FlyWire mapped every neuron in an adult fruit fly brain; Shiu et al. (Nature 2024) turned that wiring diagram into a working simulation of all 139,255 neurons. We ran that simulation ~1,000 times with every combination of the five senses above stimulated at different strengths, and your post picks the run that matches what it tastes, smells, sounds and looks like. The simulation measures MN9, the motor neuron that extends the fly's proboscis: sugar drives it, bitter suppresses it — both validated results of the paper. Brain rot is our game score: the simulated response is capped by the length, vocabulary variety and mix of bait patterns in your post. Short bait can light up the brain without earning a top score. Reaching 100% also requires activating all five senses. The 3D view is the real reconstruction, showing the neurons that actually fired, in spike order, coloured by which sense they came from. Neuron names like bluebell and good_dog are the paper's real nicknames. Scored posts are saved privately. A post only appears in the Hall of Rot when its submitter chooses to add it.

how does a post become a taste?
🍬 Sugar — humblebrags, "let that sink in", "Agree?", hashtags, one-line paragraphs, origin stories, the word "journey". Goes to the fly's sugar-sensing neurons. Sugar makes the tongue come out.
🧪 Bitter — numbers with units, long sentences, real paragraphs, "because", "however", specifics like churn or a termination clause. Goes to all 65 bitter-sensing neurons. Bitter keeps the tongue in.
👃 Smell — delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, leverage, "it's not X, it's Y", em dashes, rules of three. Goes in through the antennae. Too much and the whole brain runs away with itself.
👂 Hearing — SHOUTING, exclamation marks!!!, "LET'S GOOO". Goes to the auditory neurons in the antennae.
👁 Sight — emoji, bullet points, arrows, ticks. Goes to 800 photoreceptors. The optic lobes light up. The fly sees your post. It does not read it.
